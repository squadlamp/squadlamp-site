Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email squadlampteam@gmail.com. Please include your app version (Settings) and device, so we can reproduce the problem.

Common questions

Where is my data?

Only on your device. We have no accounts or servers, so we can't recover data from a lost or wiped device. Use Backup in the app regularly.

How do I cancel SquadLamp Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

I deleted a game by mistake

Deleted games stay in the Recycle bin for 7 days by default (adjustable in Settings, up to 30).

Common questions

Where is my data?

On your device, private to you. SquadLamp doesn't copy it anywhere on its own. To keep a safe copy, or to move to a new phone, make a backup file.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings (the gear at the top of the home screen) and tap Back up now, or tap the backup line on the home screen ("Never backed up", "Backed up today" and so on). SquadLamp creates one file with your teams and their players, tags, saved plans and scheduled games, your own formations, and your finished games, then asks where to save it: Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Saving it to a cloud drive keeps a copy even if you lose the phone. The backup doesn't include a game that is still in progress. Back up again whenever you want a fresh copy.

How do I restore, or move to a new device?

Install SquadLamp on the new device, open Settings and tap Restore or import, then pick your backup file. SquadLamp lists the games in it: tap Select all new to bring everything back, or pick only the games you want. It works between iPhone, iPad and Android. Importing adds what you pick and removes nothing else; if a game is already in the app, SquadLamp tells you before replacing it with the file's version.

The backup file contains your players' names and notes, so keep it somewhere private.

How do I cancel SquadLamp Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

I deleted a game by mistake

Deleted games stay in the Recycle bin for 7 days by default (adjustable in Settings, up to 30).

SquadLamp

Fair playing time, without the sideline juggling.

SquadLamp helps youth sports coaches plan lineups, make substitutions and keep track of who has played how much.

Built for coaches

  • Plan lineups and rotations before the game.
  • Run substitutions live, one-handed, from the sideline.
  • See playing time and fairness at a glance.
  • Keep game records, goals and notes, and share reports.

Your data stays yours

Everything you enter stays on your device. No account needed. Read the privacy policy.

SquadLamp Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

SquadLamp is an app that helps youth sports coaches plan lineups, track playing time and keep game records.

The short version: everything you put into SquadLamp stays on your device. We don't have accounts or servers. We receive something only if you email it to us: a question, feedback, or a game sent for debugging, without names (see "Emails you send us").

What SquadLamp stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored only on the device where you entered it:

  • Teams and players: names, nicknames, jersey numbers, birth year and month, preferred positions, and any notes you write about a player (for example foot preference or medical notes).
  • Games: opponents, dates, lineups, substitutions, playing time, goals, injuries and other events, plans, and your match and team notes.
  • Settings: your app preferences, such as reminders and how long deleted games are kept.

We don't see any of it unless you send it to us yourself (see "Emails you send us"), and the app doesn't send it to anyone else: a file you export goes only where you save it (see "Files you export").

What leaves your device

Purchases. SquadLamp Pro is a subscription bought through the App Store (and Google Play, once SquadLamp is on Android). We don't see your payment details; Apple or Google handles the payment. To know whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous identifier it creates for your copy of the app;
  • your purchase and subscription records from the store;
  • basic technical details needed to answer the request, such as the app version, device type, operating system and country.

RevenueCat never receives anything you enter in the app: no team, player or game data. Its handling of this information is described in its privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Links you open. Links in the app, such as this policy, the Terms of Use or your subscription settings, open in your browser or the store, under those sites' own policies.

Emails you send us. Two things in the app start an email to us, which you see and send yourself from your own email app:

  • Send feedback (in Settings) adds the app version and your device's system (for example "iOS 26.0") under your message.
  • Send game for debugging (in a game's Export options) does the same and attaches a file of that one game, so we can replay it and find what went wrong. The file has no names: players appear as "Player 1", "Player 2" and so on, and there is no team name, opponent name, venue or notes. It does contain, for that game only: each player's jersey number and a random identifier the app gave them, which means nothing outside the app; the game's date and times; and its lineups, substitutions, positions, playing time, goals, cards, injuries and other events.

As with any email, we also receive your email address. We use these emails only to answer you and to fix problems in SquadLamp, and we don't share them with anyone else. Our email account is Gmail, so Google stores them for us as our email provider, under its own privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. We delete every email you send us, with any game file attached, permanently within 90 days of receiving it. To have one deleted sooner, write to us at the address below.

Nothing else leaves your device. Files you export are saved only where you choose (see "Files you export").

What we don't collect

  • No account or sign-in. We have your email address only if you email us.
  • No analytics, usage tracking or advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No automatic crash reports: a problem reaches us only if you send it.

Files you export

SquadLamp can export reports, spreadsheets and backup files of your games. It saves them where you choose, on your device or a cloud drive such as iCloud Drive or Google Drive, and doesn't send them anywhere. What happens to a file after that is up to you: if you send it to someone, it is handled by the people and services you sent it to. A backup file contains your teams, players and games, so keep it as you would any file with your players' details.

Reminders

Plan reminders are notifications your device schedules for itself. They don't pass through any server.

Children's information

SquadLamp is for coaches and is not directed at children. Coaches may enter information about the players they coach, many of whom are children. That information stays on the coach's device. The one exception is a game a coach chooses to send us for debugging: it has no names, but it does have jersey numbers and what happened in that game, which can include an injury.

Parents or guardians who want a child's information changed or removed should ask the coach, who controls it on their device. We can't access or delete what is on the coach's device. If the coach sent us a game for debugging, the coach can ask us to delete it.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • You can edit or delete players, games and teams in the app at any time.
  • Deleted games stay in the app's Recycle bin for the time set in Settings under "Keep deleted games for" (7 days unless you change it, up to 30), then are removed for good.
  • Uninstalling SquadLamp deletes everything it stored on your device.
  • Your device's own backups (iCloud or Google) may include SquadLamp's data, depending on your device settings. Those backups are managed by Apple or Google under your account.
  • Backup files and reports you exported stay wherever you saved them.
  • Emails you send us, with any game file attached, are deleted within 90 days (see "Emails you send us").

Security

Your information is protected by your device's own security, such as its passcode and encryption. We have no servers holding your data. Emails you send us, and any game file attached, are kept in our Gmail account, which only we sign in to; Google stores them as our email provider.

Changes to this policy

If SquadLamp starts handling information differently, we'll update this policy, change its effective date, and say what changed. We'll do this before the change ships if, for example, we add optional cloud sync.
